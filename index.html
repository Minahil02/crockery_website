Our Story

Desi Panday is based in Lahore, but our copper crockery isn't made here — it comes from Gujranwala and Wazirabad, towns that have been the backbone of Pakistan's metalware industry for generations. That's where the real skill and the right equipment for copper work is.

We handpick our stock from workshops in these areas and a few other regions known for solid metalwork, checking weight, finish, and hammering quality before anything goes up on the site. Our job is sourcing the best of what's already being made there, and getting it to your door.

Because it's hand-hammered, no two pieces are perfectly identical — small variations in the hammer marks are normal, not a flaw. Order online with cash on delivery, and we're one WhatsApp message away if anything's wrong with your order.
