Our Story

Desi Panday started as a small copper workshop in Lahore, run by my grandfather. My father took it over after him, and now I run it with our small team of karigars. We still work the same way they did — no factory machines, just fire, a hammer, and a lot of practice.

Every thaal, katori and glass we make is hand-hammered, so no two pieces are ever perfectly identical — small variations in the hammer marks are normal, not a flaw. That's part of what makes it handmade instead of something bought off a shelf.

We started selling online so people outside Lahore could get real copper crockery too, without going through a middleman. Cash on delivery, and we're one WhatsApp message away if anything's wrong with your order.
