Our Story

Ẑilāl was born from a reverence for the ancients — the potters of Mesopotamia, the kiln-masters of Tang Dynasty China, the terracotta artisans of the Indus Valley.

Every bowl we shape holds memory. Every glaze carries the spirit of a technique passed down across centuries. We do not merely make crockery — we keep ancient fire alive.

Our workshop sits where the old city meets the river, and our hands follow the rhythm that hands before ours followed for ten thousand years.
